Security, Privacy & Technical FAQ

Everything you need to know about sovereign cognitive journaling.

What is the difference between Protected Vault (PV) and Nexus Mind Vault (NMV)? +
Protected Vault (PV) is your camouflaged cover partition designed for everyday use and public environments; it contains realistic benign research entries with zero private keys in RAM. Nexus Mind Vault (NMV) is your true cryptographic inner sanctum accessed through the Covert Stealth Gate, unlocking full client-side AES-GCM-256 decrypted cognitive memories and Gemini AI reflection.
What happens when I enter my Duress PIN? +
Entering the secondary Duress PIN mounts the benign Decoy Persona. The UI opens cleanly with pre-seeded realistic botanical notes without any error, countdown, or alert, providing absolute plausible deniability while leaving your real reflections cryptographically sealed.
Can Google or AI models read my private reflections? +
No. Queries sent to Gemini models are ephemeral and processed strictly in-memory through your authenticated Cloud Run proxy. No private entries are retained or used for foundation model training.
How does the Auto-Lock Inactivity feature work? +
If you remain inactive for your configured timeout period (2m, 5m, 15m, or 30m), an ambient countdown appears at 30 seconds. If not extended with Ctrl+E, the application automatically unloads all decrypted keys from RAM and returns to Protected Cover Mode.
